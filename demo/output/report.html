Method, confidence, and limitations

  • CPV mapping: "Medical X-ray imaging devices" was mapped to CPV 33111000 ("X-ray devices"), which covers medical radiography equipment; narrower codes exist for specific modalities. A different mapping changes the results; the mapping is stated so it can be challenged.
  • Scoring bands (fixed, not tuned): demand volume — contract notices in window: 0=0, 1-9=6, 10-49=12, 50-199=18, 200+=25; buyer diversity — distinct buyers in award sample: 0=0, 1=5, 2-9=10, 10-24=18, 25+=25; winner openness — top winner share of sampled awards: no awards=0, >75%=5, 50-75%=10, 25-50%=18, <25%=25; recency — days since latest contract notice: none=0, >180=5, 61-180=12, 31-60=18, <=30=25.
  • Coverage: TED lists above-EU-threshold procedures; smaller national tenders are not counted, so demand is understated, never overstated.
  • Values: awarded values are summed per currency over the sampled award notices only; framework agreements may publish ceiling values, and product CPVs can match bundled tenders where the value covers the whole project.
